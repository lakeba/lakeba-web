FUJIFILM Business Innovation New Zealand (FBNZ) announced a partnership with DoxAI, a venture of Lakeba Group, to bring Fraud Check AI, an advanced fraud detection solution designed specifically for the Financial Services Industry (FSI), to customers across New Zealand.

The partnership strengthens FBNZ's long standing commitment to helping organisations process critical documents efficiently and securely. Delivered by the FBNZ Process Automation team trusted by New Zealand businesses for decades when it comes to document management and automation, the new offering uses artificial intelligence to detect fraudulent documents with speed, accuracy, and consistency.

Headquartered in Australia, DoxAI's Fraud Check AI is designed with Australasian regulations, compliance frameworks, and real document samples in mind. This regional focus allows the solution to deliver higher accuracy and faster deployment for FSI organisations that handle large volumes of identity, financial, and compliance documentation.

"Financial institutions in New Zealand are facing increasing pressure to detect and prevent fraud while handling thousands of documents every day," said Cameron Mount, FBNZ's Director of Sales. "By partnering with DoxAI, we're expanding our capabilities and helping our customers reduce manual processes, strengthen compliance, and confidently identify fraudulent documents with AI-driven precision."

In addition to Fraud Check AI, FBNZ will be able to tap into DoxAI's wider portfolio of solutions and introduce further intelligent automation modules that can support compliance, document verification, and customer onboarding across the Legal, Education, Government, and Healthcare sectors, where data and document intensive workflows create operational bottlenecks.

"FBNZ is committed to delivering a wide suite of AI-enabled solutions to help New Zealand organisations operate more efficiently and securely," added Mount. "DoxAI's powerful roadmap of capabilities will be an important part of this, and we're excited to work with them to bring additional modules to our customers as their needs evolve."

Fraud Check AI automates the review of documents such as IDs, payslips, financial statements, and supporting paperwork, identifying inconsistencies, manipulations, and high-risk elements that may indicate fraud. This significantly reduces the administrative burden on teams who traditionally rely on time consuming manual checks.

"We are proud to collaborate with FBNZ to bring Fraud Check AI to more organisations across the country," said Alfonso Porcelli, CEO of DoxAI. "Our technology is built for the realities of the Australasian market. By combining DoxAI's accuracy with FBNZ's process automation expertise, we're delivering a powerful solution that helps financial institutions detect fraud quickly and protect their customers." The partnership aligns with FBNZ's vision for continuous innovation in business process automation. With fraud attempts becoming increasingly sophisticated, New Zealand organisations require modern tools that enhance security without compromising operational efficiency.

The integration of Fraud Check AI into FBNZ's automation portfolio allows customers to benefit from:

-   Improved fraud detection accuracy, using AI models trained on ANZ specific documents
-   Reduced manual workload, freeing teams to focus on high value tasks
-   Faster processing times, supporting better customer experiences
-   Enhanced compliance, aligned to local regulatory requirements
-   Scalable implementation, delivered by FBNZ’s trusted local experts

Fraud Check AI is now available in New Zealand exclusively through FUJIFILM Business Innovation.
