After a successful pitch to the State Library, we were informed that Lakeba had won the opportunity to develop their app. The State Library were looking for innovative, creative thinkers to provide new interfaces and interesting ways to access their collections. And thanks to Steve Roknic, Ivan Ghirlanda and Gianpaolo De Biase, we were able to conceive a good enough idea and create a working prototype, that demonstrated Lakeba was the obvious choice to build the final product. This most recent triumph comes on the back of not one, but two other successes within the competitive and challenging, yet highly rewarding apps4nsw program. Our previous achievements include Seniors Card, an app that Lakeba developed last year, and this years iHelp application.
