Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) are rapidly transforming the way we live and work. These technologies are not just about automating tasks, but about augmenting human capabilities and enabling new ways of working. By using AI and ML, we can analyse vast amounts of data, identify patterns and industry trends, making predictions that would be time consuming and subject constrained for humans to do on their own.

At DoxAI, we are at the forefront of this revolution, helping businesses to reduce costs and increase margins, improve performance and customer satisfaction, and scale business growth.

One of the primary areas we focus on is using AI and ML to automate manual processes. The current DoxAI marketplace offers a wide selection of services that can help businesses streamline their operations and improve efficiency. This includes services such as identity verification, document and information cross-checking, automated data or document classification and categorisation, and data extraction from documents or images.

The identity verification service allows businesses to quickly and easily verify the identity of their customers, employees, or partners using a range of methods, including facial recognition, document verification, and biometric authentication. In addition to speeding up the process, saving time and resources, and improving customer satisfaction, using AI in this way improves security and mitigates associated risks.

DoxAI’s document and information cross-checking service is also a valuable tool for businesses. By using AI and ML, we are able to quickly and accurately cross-check information from a range of sources, such as documents, images, and databases. This can help to improve the accuracy of data and reduce the risk of errors at the point of execution instead of when it may be to late to act, making the process of information verification more efficient and reliable.

The flexible automated data and document classification and categorisation service allows businesses to efficiently and cost effectively manage large amounts of information or material.

Alongside this, we are also using AI and ML to extract data from documents and images. This is typically an expensive, time-consuming, and error-prone process for business to do manually. With DoxAI’s, service, information can be extracted quickly and accurately from a range of different formats, such as PDFs, images, and handwritten documents.
