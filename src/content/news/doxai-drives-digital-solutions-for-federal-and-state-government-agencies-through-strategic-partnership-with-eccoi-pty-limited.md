DoxAI is proud to announce a new strategic partnership with Canberra-based IT consultancy and recruitment firm, Eccoi Pty Limited. This collaboration is set to provide digital solutions to Federal and State Government agencies Australia-wide, simplifying workflow automation and delivering cutting-edge AI-powered systems across government sectors. 

Eccoi Pty Limited, well-established in serving Federal and State Government agencies, will be a key partner in our innovative solutions across the Australian Federal, State and Local Government sector. Their extensive expertise and strong client relationships lay the groundwork for efficiently deploying DoxAI’s services. The partnership further strengthens both companies' positions on the DTA Software Marketplace Panel for ERP Products and Services, as mandated by the Australian Digital Transformation Agency. 

DoxAI's clients will benefit from this collaboration by gaining access to Eccoi's vast network of over 400 consultants, Australia-wide, enabling faster integration and implementation of DoxAI’s AI-driven solutions. With a focus on digital transformation, this partnership offers Australian Government agencies tailored solutions that enhance workflow automation and efficiency. 

![](./images/doxai-drives-digital-solutions-for-federal-and-state-government-agencies-through-strategic-partnership-with-eccoi-pty-limited/image-1.webp)

Adrian Vallino, Managing Director DoxAI says:  
***"Partnering with Eccoi enables us to combine DoxAI's cutting-edge AI technology with their deep-rooted expertise in the Federal and State Government sectors. This collaboration will accelerate the delivery of tailored digital transformation solutions that streamline operations and improve efficiency across public services. Together, we’re set to drive innovation and deliver meaningful impact to government agencies, empowering them to embrace the future of AI-powered workflow automation."***

Sam Bartone, Co-founder and Chief Consultant of Eccoi *says: **"Our partnership with DoxAI represents a significant step forward in our ability to provide government agencies with state-of-the-art digital solutions. By integrating DoxAI’s advanced AI technology with our local expertise and deep understanding of client needs, we can offer even more efficient, tailored services to help streamline operations across the Federal and State Government sectors. This collaboration strengthens our position as leaders in digital transformation, driving successful outcomes for our clients."***

Through this partnership, Eccoi can now offer its clients access to top-tier consultancy services paired with DoxAI’s advanced AI solutions. This will result in faster, more specialised digital transformation consultancy, delivering rapid results and greater project success for clients seeking to enhance their digital operations. 

The collaboration between DoxAI and Eccoi opens up new opportunities for delivering AI-driven solutions across various sectors, including financial services, real estate, insurance, and professional services. Backed by their recent success on the DTA Category 3 ERP Products and Services, Whole of Government panel, both companies are poised to drive innovation, improve workflow processes, and support continued growth in scalable technology solutions for the public sector.
