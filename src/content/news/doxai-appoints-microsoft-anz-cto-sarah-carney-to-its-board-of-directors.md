Sydney, Australia 10 October 2024

DoxAI announces the appointment of Sarah Carney as a non-executive director to its board. Sarah is Microsoft's National Chief Technology Officer in Australia and New Zealand, and she brings a wealth of expertise in technology and business strategy to the role.

In her position at Microsoft, Sarah is responsible for identifying and understanding emerging technical trends, developing strategies to leverage these trends, and guiding major customers in the enterprise and government sectors on adopting cutting-edge technologies. Her role also encompasses leading Responsible AI initiatives and contributing to Microsoft’s regional AI product Red Team.

Before joining Microsoft, Sarah had a distinguished career that included service in the Australian army, extensive international experience across Europe and the Middle East, and a diverse range of roles, from supporting think tanks to working in custom business applications and even ballet.

Sarah holds a Bachelor of Science from the University of NSW, a Postgraduate Diploma in Marketing from Cambridge Marketing College, and an MBA from the City of London University. Her dedication to education and learning is evident in her advisory roles with the Institute of Applied Technology—Digital, Swinburne University, and Chisholm TAFE. In 2024, B&T recognised her as the fifth most powerful woman in technology in Australia.

![](./images/doxai-appoints-microsoft-anz-cto-sarah-carney-to-its-board-of-directors/image-1.webp)

Sarah expressed her enthusiasm about joining DoxAI, stating, ***“I am excited to be joining DoxAI as a Non-Executive Board Director. I look forward to bringing my technology expertise and business knowledge to the team and contributing to the company’s innovative journey. Together, we will continue to push the boundaries of what is possible and drive impactful change.”***

Giuseppe Porcelli, Executive Chairman of DoxAI and Group CEO of Lakeba, says: ***"We are delighted to welcome Sarah to the DoxAI board. Her leadership in the tech industry, combined with her expertise in AI and cloud computing, will be invaluable as we continue to innovate and expand our AI solutions."***

![](./images/doxai-appoints-microsoft-anz-cto-sarah-carney-to-its-board-of-directors/image-2.webp)

![](./images/doxai-appoints-microsoft-anz-cto-sarah-carney-to-its-board-of-directors/image-3.webp)

Kevin Wo, Microsoft Asia CEO and Lakeba Board member, also commented:  
***“Congratulations on your appointment to the DoxAI Board, Sarah! We are thrilled to welcome you to the DoxAI Board. Your expertise and outside-in perspectives will undoubtedly play a crucial role in accelerating DoxAI's growth and expanding its*** ***market presence. We look forward to achieving great milestones together.”***

DoxAI is confident that Sarah’s exceptional experience and visionary leadership will significantly contribute to the company’s continued success and growth.
