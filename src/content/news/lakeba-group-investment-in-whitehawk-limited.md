Lakeba Group Limited (“Lakeba” or the “Company”) is pleased to announce a strategic investment in *WhiteHawk Limited*(ASX: WHK) (“WhiteHawk”), a global technology company focused on AI-driven cybersecurity and digital risk management solutions.

Under a completed placement (Tranche 2), Lakeba has acquired an equity interest in WhiteHawk by subscribing for ordinary shares and attaching listed options, resulting in Lakeba becoming a **top 10 shareholder** in WhiteHawk. The investment underscores Lakeba’s confidence in WhiteHawk’s platform, management and growth strategy in cybersecurity and AI-enabled risk solutions. ([Intelligent Investor](https://www.intelligentinvestor.com.au/shares/asx-whk/whitehawk-limited/announcements?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

**Key Investment Highlights**

-   Lakeba has been issued **18,260,870 fully paid ordinary WhiteHawk shares** and **9,130,435 WHKOA listed options** as part of the Tranche 2 placement. ([Intelligent Investor](https://www.intelligentinvestor.com.au/shares/asx-whk/whitehawk-limited/announcements?utm_source=chatgpt.com))
-   The listed options (WHKOA) are exercisable on the terms applicable to WhiteHawk’s quoted options. ([Intelligent Investor](https://www.intelligentinvestor.com.au/shares/asx-whk/whitehawk-limited/announcements?utm_source=chatgpt.com))
-   Upon completion of the transaction, Lakeba Group is among the **top 10 shareholders** in WhiteHawk by shareholding. ([Intelligent Investor](https://www.intelligentinvestor.com.au/shares/asx-whk/whitehawk-limited/announcements?utm_source=chatgpt.com))
-   The investment reflects endorsement of WhiteHawk’s strategic direction, accelerating its global expansion, and enhancing capability in key markets including North America, Asia-Pacific and the Middle East. ([Intelligent Investor](https://www.intelligentinvestor.com.au/shares/asx-whk/whitehawk-limited/announcements?utm_source=chatgpt.com))
-   Lakeba brings strong experience in scaling technology ventures and strategic partnerships, particularly in AI, innovation ecosystems, and emerging regions. ([Intelligent Investor](https://www.intelligentinvestor.com.au/shares/asx-whk/whitehawk-limited/announcements?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

**Strategic Rationale**

The investment aligns with Lakeba’s strategy to support high-growth technology companies operating in markets with significant potential. WhiteHawk’s cloud-native, AI/ML-driven cybersecurity exchange and risk mitigation platform demonstrates strong relevance in the evolving global cybersecurity landscape. Lakeba’s involvement is intended to support WhiteHawk’s working capital needs, international market development, and long-term value creation for shareholders.

**Information Relating to Securities Issued**

Lakeba was issued ordinary shares and attaching WHKOA listed options under the terms of WhiteHawk’s placement; no further consideration is payable by Lakeba in respect of the attached listed options. The shares and options are subject to the customary terms and conditions applicable to WhiteHawk securities. ([Intelligent Investor](https://www.intelligentinvestor.com.au/shares/asx-whk/whitehawk-limited/announcements?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

**About WhiteHawk**

WhiteHawk Limited (ASX: WHK) operates a cloud-based cybersecurity exchange that enables organisations of all sizes to assess, prioritise and mitigate digital risk using automated, scalable AI-driven tools. The platform is designed to make cyber risk management more accessible and efficient globally.
